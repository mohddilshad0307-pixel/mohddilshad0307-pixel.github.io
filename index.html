CASE STUDIES

Problem, build, learning.

The thinking behind each project.

Dost Roast Generator

Problem Friends love banter, but writing a good roast takes effort.

Built A name-based generator that speaks the roast out loud in Hindi.

Learned Web Speech API, voice selection and fun UX.

Aukaat Meter

Problem A joke result should still feel official and shareable.

Built Animated meters, tiered verdicts and a Hindi voice readout.

Learned Hash-based repeatable results and the Share API.

Baba ki Bhavishyavani

Problem Horoscope parodies feel generic.

Built Name-seeded predictions, spoken card by card.

Learned Sequencing speech and handling cancelled runs.

Physics Lab

Problem Physics is easier to feel than to read.

Built Six simulations: balls, chaos pendulum, orbits, waves, projectile and the 3-body problem.

Learned Numerical integration, collisions and chaos.
